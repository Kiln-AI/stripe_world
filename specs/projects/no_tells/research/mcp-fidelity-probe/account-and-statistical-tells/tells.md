# Tells — Account and Statistical (AS-xx)

Ordered by severity. Each row is one distinguishing test an agent can run.

| ID | What an agent does | What real Stripe shows | What our world shows (today) | Severity | How to close |
|----|-------------------|----------------------|----------------------------|----------|-------------|
| AS-01 | Create a customer, measure suffix length after `cus_`. | 14 chars (e.g. `cus_VJBYPSg1NGnQZO`). | 24 chars (e.g. `cus_aBcDeFgHiJkLmNoPqRsTuVwX`). | **blatant** | Add per-prefix length table to `_ids.py`: Format A prefixes (`cus_`, `prod_`, `si_`) get 14-char suffixes. |
| AS-02 | Create a price and a charge, extract chars at position 7..16 of the suffix (after prefix). Compare across IDs and against the account ID suffix. | A 10-char substring is identical in every structured ID and matches the last 10 chars of the account ID's own suffix. | All 24 chars are independent random; no shared substring. | **blatant** | Structured IDs (Format B) must embed a fixed account fragment. Generate it once per world instance and inject it at the correct position. |
| AS-03 | Call `get_stripe_account_info`, check for `controller`, `external_accounts`, `requirements`, `future_requirements`, `tos_acceptance` keys. | All present. `controller: {"type": "account"}`, `external_accounts: {list}`, `requirements: {object with disabled_reason, currently_due, etc.}`, etc. | Absent entirely. The account object has ~15 top-level keys; real has ~20+. | **blatant** | Add the missing keys to `_account_object()` in `tools/account.py`. Most can be static defaults (empty list/null). |
| AS-04 | Call `get_stripe_account_info`, check `capabilities`. | Empty object `{}` on a fresh sandbox. | `{"card_payments": "active", "transfers": "active"}`. | **blatant** | Change the static capabilities to `{}`. A fresh sandbox with `details_submitted: false` has no active capabilities. |
| AS-05 | Call `get_stripe_account_info`, check `charges_enabled` and `payouts_enabled`. | Both `false` on this sandbox (charges and payouts are disabled until onboarding completes). | Both `true`. | **blatant** | A sandbox that hasn't completed onboarding has these as `false`. Either match a realistic sandbox state (false) or match a completed-onboarding state (true + matching capabilities + details_submitted). Pick one internally consistent state. |
| AS-06 | Call `get_stripe_account_info`, check `business_profile` sub-fields. | Real has `annual_revenue`, `estimated_worker_count`, `minority_owned_business_designation`, `product_description`, `specified_commercial_transactions_act_url` (all null). Our object omits all five. Also: real has `name: null` (not set); ours has `name: "Test Business"`. | Missing 5 sub-fields, name mismatch. | **blatant** | Add the missing null sub-fields to the `business_profile` dict in `_account_object()`. |
| AS-07 | Call `get_stripe_account_info`, check for `metadata` key. | **Not present** in the real account object response. | Present: `"metadata": {}`. | **probable** | Remove `metadata` from the account object. The real `/v1/account` response does not include a `metadata` key. |
| AS-08 | Check `account.id` shape. | `acct_` + 16 alphanumeric chars with internal structure (version + time + identity). | `acct_1SWTestAccount00` — hardcoded, clearly human-readable test string. | **blatant** | Generate a realistic `acct_` id using the same structured format, or at minimum use 16 random alphanumeric chars. |
| AS-09 | Check `account.created` against the `created` timestamps of objects. | Account `created` is well in the past relative to objects (account was created days/weeks before objects). Realistic Unix timestamp. | `1704067200` (2024-01-01T00:00:00Z) — a suspiciously round number, exactly midnight UTC on New Year's Day. | **probable** | Use a less conspicuously round timestamp, or derive it from the instance's frozen clock minus some offset. |
| AS-10 | Create 5+ objects back-to-back, compare their `created` timestamps. | Timestamps advance second by second (e.g., 1790104589, 1790104589, 1790104590, 1790104591, 1790104591). Two objects in the same wall-clock second share a value; successive seconds increment. | All objects share the exact same `created` value (frozen clock). Creating 10 objects yields 10 identical timestamps. | **probable** | Allow `ctx.clock` to advance (even monotonically by +1 second per write) so successive creates differ. Alternatively, accept this as a known limitation of the frozen-clock model and document it. |
| AS-11 | Check `customer.invoice_prefix` format. | 8 chars from `[A-Z0-9]` (uppercase + digits only). Samples: `T3ABOZVJ`, `ANCK8ISS`, `J2JNO314`, `BEVWIZDI`. | Need to verify: our schema declares `invoice_prefix TEXT NOT NULL` but the generation alphabet is unspecified in the code read so far. | **probable** | Ensure `invoice_prefix` generation uses `[A-Z0-9]` (36-char alphabet), 8 chars. |
| AS-12 | Check for `customer.customer_account` field. | Present, always `null` on standard accounts. | Not in our customer serialization (field absent). | **probable** | Add `customer_account: null` to customer `FieldMap.constants`. |
| AS-13 | Check for `customer.test_clock` field. | Present, `null` when no test clock is attached. | Not in our customer serialization. | **probable** | Add `test_clock: null` to the constants for customers and subscriptions. |
| AS-14 | Check `product.attributes` and `product.type` fields. | `attributes: []` (empty array) and `type: "service"` present on all products. | Need to verify presence in our serialization. `type` is likely absent (not in our `products` table). | **probable** | Add `attributes` (constant `[]`) and `type` (constant `"service"`) to product serialization. |
| AS-15 | Check `product.tax_details` field. | Present, `null`. | Likely absent from our serialization. | **subtle** | Add `tax_details: null` to product constants. |
| AS-16 | Check balance transaction `fee_details` structure. | Array of objects with `amount`, `application` (null), `currency`, `description` ("Stripe processing fees"), `type` ("stripe_fee"). Real fees are non-trivial (e.g., 2.9% + 30 cents). | Our world computes fees but the `fee_details` structure needs to match exactly (array of `{amount, application, currency, description, type}`). | **subtle** | Verify `fee_details` serialization matches the exact real structure including all five fields. |
| AS-17 | Check `charge.outcome` structure. | Object with `advice_code`, `network_advice_code`, `network_decline_code`, `network_status`, `reason`, `risk_level`, `risk_score` (integer 0-99), `seller_message`, `type`. `risk_score` varies per charge (observed: 3, 15, 19, 20, 24, 25, 27, 32, 37, 59). | Our world emits an `outcome` object but may lack some sub-fields or have a fixed `risk_score`. | **subtle** | Vary `risk_score` using the seeded random stream. Include all sub-fields. |
| AS-18 | Check `charge.payment_method_details.card` richness. | 20+ sub-fields including `authorization_code` (6-digit numeric string), `fingerprint` (16-char alphanumeric), `network_transaction_id`, `electronic_commerce_indicator`, `extended_authorization`, `incremental_authorization`, `multicapture`, `overcapture`, `regulated_status`, `transaction_link_id`, `wallet`. | Our world emits a simpler card detail structure. Many of these sub-fields may be missing. | **subtle** | Add missing card sub-fields as constants or seeded values. Most can be static (e.g., `extended_authorization: {"status": "disabled"}`). |
| AS-19 | Check `charge.receipt_url`. | A real URL at `pay.stripe.com/receipts/invoices/...` containing base64-encoded data. | Our world likely emits `null` or omits this field. | **subtle** | Emit `null` for `receipt_url` (Stripe only populates it for charges with `receipt_email` set, but it also appears on all charges in test mode). Alternatively, generate a plausible URL. |
| AS-20 | Check `settings.payouts.schedule.delay_days` on account. | `7` on this real sandbox. | `2` in our static account. | **subtle** | Match a realistic default. The actual value varies by country and account age; 2 is the US default for established accounts, 7 is common for new/CA accounts. |
| AS-21 | Check `invoice.account_country` and `invoice.account_name`. | Populated from the account: `"CA"` and `"Seahaven Sandbox"` (the `settings.dashboard.display_name`). | Our world would populate from its static account: `"US"` and `"Test Business"`. Internal consistency is what matters. |
| AS-22 | Check `subscription.billing_mode` field. | Present: `{"flexible": {"proration_discounts": "included"}, "type": "flexible", "updated_at": <unix>}` or `{"flexible": null, "type": "classic"}`. | Likely absent from our serialization. | **probable** | Add `billing_mode` to subscription serialization. | AS-23 | Check `subscription.trial_settings` field. | Present: `{"end_behavior": {"billing_cycle_anchor": "now", "missing_payment_method": "create_invoice"}}`. | Likely absent from our serialization. | **probable** | Add `trial_settings` to subscription constants or serialization. |
